Test session

Thank you for trying this prototype. It is an early version of two things: the tool our planners use to run a wedding, and the private page each couple gets. We would like to see how it feels to use, before we build it for real.

Open the prototype

Before you start

  • Sign in with the login and password you were given. Then do the six goals for your role, in order: about 20 minutes.
  • Please say out loud, or note down, what you expect and where you hesitate. That is the most useful thing you can give us.
  • There are no wrong moves. If something is hard to find, the design is at fault, not you.
  • Do not take the tour yet. We first want to see how clear things are without any help.
  • Everything is invented: people, suppliers and prices. "Today" inside the prototype is always Monday 17 May 2027.
  • You cannot break anything. "Reset demo data" on the page where you choose a person puts it all back.

Then take the tour

Go back to the page where you chose a person and start the tour for your role. It takes two minutes. Afterwards: what became clearer, and what would you have liked to know from the start?

Five last questions

  1. What would you open every day?
  2. What did you look for and not find?
  3. Was there anything you did not understand, or did not quite believe?
  4. If we could change one thing first, what should it be?
  5. Planners: could this replace the tools you use now? Couples: would this make you feel looked after?

Please send your notes to the person who invited you. Demo data: every person, supplier and price is invented.
